# Chalo Chhath Ghat (चलो छठ घाट)
### 600-Meter Pilgrimage Journey — Anime Aesthetic & Realistic Showcase

An online, real-time 3D browser pilgrimage and crowd game capturing the authentic soul of **Chhath Puja Mahaparv** from the village ancestral home to the holy Ganges.

---

## Overview

### 1. The Full 600-Meter Pilgrimage Route (घर से गंगा घाट तक)
The ghat is not right outside your door—it is a **600-meter devotional journey** through 5 distinct living zones:
* **0m to 120m — Ancestral Home & Courtyard (घर का आंगन व तुलसी चौरा)**:
  - Traditional brick village house with sloping clay tiled roof (*khaprail makaan*), carved wooden doors, and verandah.
  - Sacred clay *Tulsi Chaura* with burning diya.
  - Mango-wood river-clay stove (*Mitti ka Chulha*) with rising smoke where Kharna kheer and Thekua are freshly prepared.
  - Lifting the heavy bamboo Daura onto the head.
* **120m to 280m — Village Alleys & Basti (गाँव की पगडंडी व बस्ती)**:
  - Mud-walled thatched huts, cow sheds (*bathan*), stacked cow-dung fuel cakes (*goitha*).
  - Devotees performing *Dandvat Pranaam* (crawling prostrations with sacred chalk lines).
  - Villagers offering pranaam and touching the Daura for blessings.
* **280m to 440m — Festive Bazaar & Toran Dwar (तोरण द्वार व सहरिया बाजार)**:
  - Grand illuminated welcome archway (*Chhath Mahaparv Toran Dwar*).
  - Bunting garland flags strung across the road.
  - Festival market stalls selling sugarcane bundles, giant pomelos (*Dabh Nimbu*), water chestnuts (*Singhara*), and clay lamps.
  - Loudspeaker pole broadcasting Padma Bhushan **Sharda Sinha's** immortal folk melodies.
* **440m to 540m — Sandy Floodplain Trail (रेत व बालू का कछार)**:
  - Road transitions into soft river sand (*balu*).
  - Flanked by lush banana tree groves with hanging banana hands.
  - Winter morning mist (*kuhaasa*) gathering over the trail.
* **540m to 600m+ — The Grand Sacred Ganga Ghat (महा पावन गंगा घाट)**:
  - Multi-tiered red sandstone steps descending into the water.
  - 5-sugarcane *Kosi* canopies with terracotta elephants (*Kosi Haathi*) and burning oil lamps.
  - The Holy Ganges river with real-time mirror reflections (`MeshReflectorMaterial`).
  - Devotees standing waist-deep in water offering holy Arghya to the blazing sun.

### 2. Studio Ghibli / Makoto Shinkai Anime-Style People
* **Anime Vrati (Heroine Devotee)**:
  - Natural anime human anatomy: expressive eyes with specular highlights, tapered jawline, styled dark hair with marigolds.
  - Draped cotton-silk *Piliya* saree with crimson borders and golden zari embroidery.
  - **Billowing Saree Pallu**: Graceful cloth tail waving and fluttering in the river wind with procedural wave animation.
  - Iconic **Orange Matiya Sindoor** drawn cleanly from the tip of the nose up the forehead parting.
  - Jointed arms holding the woven bamboo *Soop* with coconut, dabh pomelo, thekua, and a lit diya.
* **Anime Daura Bearer (Lead Protagonist)**:
  - Athletic anime hero build with styled dark anime hair and devotional tilak.
  - Draped white kurta with knee-jointed walking legs.
  - Saffron-yellow *gamchha* fluttering over the shoulder in the breeze.
  - Hands raised supporting the large bamboo *Daura* on a twisted cloth ring (*bitha*).
  - Biomechanical walking cycle: hip sway, knee flexion, foot plant, and balanced basket sway.

### 3. Realistic & Anime-Detailed Banana Trees (Kelwa ke Ped)
* **Organic Tapered Trunk**: Curved pseudostem with overlapping leaf sheath textures and dried peeling bark layers.
* **10 Sweeping Drooping Fronds**: Modeled with realistic parabolic curves, pale green central midribs, high-resolution parallel vein textures, and natural wind-split slits in the leaves.
* **Hanging Banana Bunch (*Kelwa ke Ghaudh*)**: 4 spiral tiers of curved crescent finger bananas shifting from yellow to green, terminating in a deep burgundy banana blossom (*Mocha*).
* **Bamboo Support Prop (*Theka*)**: Angled pole supporting the heavy fruit branch.

### 4. 150+ Devotees Massive Crowd & Moving Family Kafila
The pilgrimage and ghat are filled with a complete community of devotees celebrating together:
* **12+ Accompanying Family Kafila (परिवार संग यात्रा)**:
  - You are never alone when walking the 600-meter pilgrimage route or starting at home.
  - **Vrati Mother (छठी व्रती माँ)**: Walking right alongside holding the bamboo soop with coconut, pomelo, and glowing diya.
  - **Little Kids (छोटू व मुन्नी)**: Chintu skipping joyously carrying a miniature sugarcane stalk, and Munni in a festive ghaghra-choli with a brass flower thali.
  - **Dada ji & Dadi ji**: Dada ji with his wooden walking stick and woollen shawl, and Dadi ji singing traditional folk melodies.
  - **Brothers & Relatives**: Brother carrying a 5-sugarcane bundle, dholak drum player, fragrant smoking dhunuchi burner, bhabhi carrying the brass Gangajal kalash, and second daura bearer.
* **Continuous 600m Marching Pilgrims**:
  - Over 45 pilgrims distributed from 0m Home, Village Basti, Festive Bazaar, and Sandy Trail continuously walk in dynamic motion toward the Ganga Ghat.
  - Never frozen or static; each pilgrim walks with natural biomechanical stride and body bobbing, seamlessly continuing their pilgrimage.
* **Seated Devotee Circles on Ghat Steps (बैठे हुए श्रद्धालु व परिजन)**:
  - **Women Bhajan Mandali**: Seated cross-legged in circles on jute carpets singing traditional Chhath songs around brass offering thalis.
  - **Elders Gathering**: Grandfathers and uncles sitting wrapped in shawls on blankets, drinking tea from terracotta kulhads and conversing.
  - **Cousins & Kids Guarding Dauras**: Seated along the stone steps, watching over fresh bamboo baskets and sugarcane canopies.
  - **Vratis in Dhyana**: Seated in quiet prayer near the water edge before stepping into the sacred river.
* **Dynamic Wandering & Strolling People (घूमते-टहलते श्रद्धालु)**:
  - Devotees strolling along the ghat promenade, greeting relatives with folded hands.
  - Mobile tea vendor (Chai-wallah) carrying a brass kettle serving hot tea.
  - Youngsters taking scenic photographs of the sunset and sunrise across the Ganges.
* **36 River Wading Vratis**: Standing waist-deep in the holy Ganges in two respectful rows, holding bamboo soops with lit diyas facing the Sun.
* **24 Water Arghya Helpers**: Sons and family members standing beside vratis holding brass kalashes and pouring cascading milk arghya streams.

### 5. Firecrackers, Sparklers & Skyrocket Celebrations (पटाखे, फुलझड़ी व आतिशबाजी)
Authentic festive atmosphere capturing the joyous sound and light of the riverbanks:
* **Handheld Phuljhari (फुलझड़ी)**: Children and youngsters waving sparklers in hand with glittering particle trails and warm point lights.
* **Terracotta Anaar Fountains (अनार)**: Ground fountain cones shooting sparkling golden-silver sprays 4 meters high into the air.
* **Ground Chakris (चकरी)**: Rapidly spinning wheel fireworks casting radial sparkling rings on the sandstone steps.
* **Aerial Skyrocket Fireworks (आसमानी रॉकेट)**: Continuous rockets launching high into the sky over the Ganges (Z: 280 to 330), bursting into expansive multi-colored spherical starbursts (crimson, emerald, gold, cyan) with synthesized whistling launch and explosion audio.
* **Interactive Launch [Key P]**: Devotees can launch aerial skyrockets anytime from the HUD or keyboard shortcut.

### 6. Celestial Sun & Real-time Arghya Time Selection (संध्या व उषा अर्घ्य)
Dedicated time switcher to experience both divine rituals with accurate atmospheric lighting:
* **Sandhya Arghya (Dhalti Surya / Sunset)**:
  - Deep crimson-gold setting sun resting right on the Ganges horizon (Y: 11, Z: 335).
  - Warm evening twilight sky with long golden specular reflection trail across the river.
  - Amber sunset illumination bathing devotees and ghat steps.
* **Usha Arghya (Ugte Surya / Sunrise)**:
  - Radiant golden morning sun rising above the river (Y: 19, Z: 335).
  - Twelve sacred sunbeams (द्वादश सूर्य रश्मि) emanating outward with pulsating solar corona.
  - Morning river mist (kuhaasa) with diamond glimmer reflections on the sacred waters.
* **Instant Toggle [Key T]**: Smoothly switch between Sandhya Arghya and Usha Arghya with one click.

---

### 7. Universal Blessings, Charan Sparsh & Paran Ceremony
An interactive ceremony capturing the deep emotional culmination of the festival:
* **Charan Sparsh (Touching Feet of Elders & Vratis)**: Devotees touch the feet of the fasting Vrati mother, Dada ji, and Dadi ji to receive heartfelt Bhojpuri blessings (*"जुग जुग जिया बबुआ! छठी मइया तोहार सब मनोरथ सिद्ध करसु!"*).
* **Matiya Sindoor Daan**: Married women apply deep orange vermilion from the tip of the nose up to the parting of the hairline on each other.
* **Mahaprasad Thekua Sharing**: Distributing freshly made ghee thekua to 100+ devotees gathered on the ghat.
* **Paran (Breaking Fast)**: Concluding the 36-hour waterless fast with sacred Gangajal and raw cow milk.

## Tech Stack

* **3D Rendering**: Three.js, React Three Fiber, React Three Drei
* **Shaders & Reflections**: MeshReflectorMaterial, MeshToonMaterial with procedural cel-shading gradient maps
* **Frontend**: React 18, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS
* **Audio**: Web Audio API Procedural Synthesizer (Shankhnaad, temple bells, blessing chimes, dholak beats, tanpura drone)
